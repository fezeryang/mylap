## Suitability verdict

Conditional pass for a hero-quality, real-time browser prop. The 1536 × 1024 source isolates one

- Primary type: portable cassette radio / boombox with a telescopic aerial and parabolic dish.
- Domain: object; hard-surface mechanical assembly with repeated modules and articulated controls.
- Confidence: 0.99.

### 2. Overall form and silhouette

- The chassis is a wide chamfered cuboid, approximately 1.55:1 width-to-height and about 0.34 of
  its width in inferred depth.

### 5. PBR materials

- Main shell: pearl-painted metal/composite, dielectric base, roughness 0.22–0.34, clearcoat
  0.55–0.75, with cool lavender reflection and restrained micro-grain.
- Structural trim and dials: brushed aluminium, metalness 0.9–1.0, roughness 0.16–0.28,
  anisotropy aligned around revolved parts.
- Bezel and grille: near-black navy dielectric/painted metal, roughness 0.28–0.5.
- Tuner and cassette glass: blue-violet tinted dielectric, roughness 0.06–0.14, clearcoat 1.0;
  transmission is kept low so live display content remains saturated and readable.
- Handle grip: satin lavender polymer, roughness 0.34–0.45, subtle normal grain.
- Emissive accents: cyan, electric blue, violet, and magenta with modest intensity so they colour
  nearby trim without clipping to pure white.
