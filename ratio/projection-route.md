# Surface Projection Decision

Route: **procedural PBR materials plus live semantic displays**, not photo projection.

The source is a stylized hero render with strong baked cyan/magenta lighting. Projecting its pixels
onto the model would double-light the pearl metal, lock the result to one camera, and turn future
music information and transport controls into inert imagery. The front tuner, analog meter,
spectrum, cassette state, frequency labels, and status indicators therefore remain independently
addressable live surfaces backed by typed media state.

Physical shell, trim, speaker perforations, buttons, knobs, antenna, and dish are procedural
geometry with separate PBR channels. Small static inscriptions use generated semantic labels where
they help identity. The reference remains review evidence only and is never shipped as a screenshot
texture or full-surface albedo shortcut.
