# Neon Cyberphone Reference Analysis

## Suitability verdict

Conditional pass for a hero-quality real-time browser prop. The 1228 × 1228 source has one
unoccluded target, a strong three-quarter silhouette, a neutral transparent/black surround, and
legible front-glass, left-rail, and bottom-edge materials. The rear shell, right rail, and exact
camera projection are hidden, so those regions remain evidence-labelled inferences rather than
exact reconstructions.

## Layered observation

### 1. Identification

- Primary type: modern slab smartphone in a transparent protective bumper.
- Domain: object; hard-surface, layered-shell, articulated only at side controls.
- Confidence: 0.98.

### 2. Overall form and silhouette

- Tall rounded cuboid with an approximately 2.05:1 face aspect ratio and bilateral base geometry.
- The visible screen is a shallow convex glass plane inset inside three nested perimeter bands.
- The reference camera sees the front, left lateral rail, and bottom edge; the top recedes away.
- The phone leans clockwise in image space, but object-space remains portrait with +Y toward top.

### 3. Macro, meso, micro hierarchy

- Macro: front glass/display, metallic chassis, transparent bumper, hidden rear shell.
- Meso: nested screen bezels, lower speaker pair, USB-C port, left side buttons, antenna seams,
  punch-hole camera, cyan and magenta emissive edge strips.
- Micro: speaker perforation rows, port inner tongue, camera lens rings, button knurling, rim
  highlights, glass sparkle, hairline panel seams, and screen star speckles.

### 4. Spatial relationships

- Display glass is inset flush within the black inner bezel.
- Black bezel overlaps the metallic chassis; the clear bumper sockets around the chassis perimeter.
- Side buttons are embedded into the left lateral bumper and stand slightly proud.
- USB-C receptacle is recessed into the bottom chassis; speaker holes are embedded beside it.
- Camera lens is embedded through the front display near the top centre.

### 5. PBR materials

- Display glass: dielectric, near-black/navy albedo, roughness 0.08–0.16, clearcoat 1.0,
  transmission kept low enough for the emissive UI to remain saturated.
- Chassis: silver-blue polished metal, metalness 0.92–1.0, roughness 0.16–0.28, anisotropic
  brushing inferred from the long edge highlights.
- Bumper: clear blue-violet polymer, transmission 0.75–0.9, IOR about 1.46, roughness 0.12–0.2.
- Bezel/port interiors: opaque near-black dielectric, roughness 0.22–0.42.
- Light strips and screen: emissive cyan, electric blue, violet, and magenta; bloom is a renderer
  response rather than baked albedo.

### 6. Colour and finish

- Screen base: near-black navy → saturated cobalt vertical gradient.
- Primary luminous ramp: cyan (#28e8ff) → electric blue (#315cff) → violet (#8b45ff) → magenta
  (#ff42ef), used selectively on rim state and UI emphasis.
- Metal ramps from cool silver through blue-violet reflections; no pure-white body fill.
- The clear bumper inherits cyan on the right/bottom and magenta on the top/left from emissive rims.

### 7. Identity-defining systems

1. Triple-layer rounded perimeter with alternating black, polished silver, and clear neon rim.
2. Edge-to-edge cobalt display and central punch-hole camera.
3. Bottom USB-C port with cyan/magenta illuminated surround and paired speaker perforations.
4. Three left-side controls, including an elongated textured central button.
5. Dense cyberpunk dashboard: 09:42 clock, skyline/orb hero, weather/music panels, four app tiles,
   5G graph/globe panel, and swipe affordance.

### 8. Unknowns and confidence

- Rear geometry and finish: hidden; inferred as a restrained dark glass/metal back, confidence 0.35.
- Right-side control layout: hidden; left unadorned unless later reference evidence is supplied,
  confidence 0.25.
- Exact physical dimensions: inferred from a contemporary phone ratio, confidence 0.65.
- Exact UI glyph font and generated skyline pixels: approximated procedurally/with semantic DOM,
  confidence 0.7; interaction structure takes priority over baking the screenshot as one image.
- Apparent clear-case protrusions at the top corners are partially clipped, confidence 0.55.

## Quality target

Visible-view goal is a strong procedural real-time match (target 0.85): correct silhouette,
layered thickness, chrome/clear/glass response, neon palette, bottom hardware, side controls, and
recognizable screen hierarchy. Hidden-side claims are intentionally capped below near-reference
confidence until additional front/side/rear photos exist.
