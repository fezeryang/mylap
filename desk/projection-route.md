# Surface fidelity route

Route: **procedural finish**, exactness: **image-only approximation**.

The reference contains mostly solid coated metal, dark composite and emissive acrylic rather than a
unique painted photographic pattern. The work-surface HUD is interactive/reusable linework whose
geometry and state must remain independently addressable. Therefore the source raster is not baked
onto the desk. Colors are sampled and material response is inferred from verified crops; the HUD,
rails, controls and status lamps are authored as geometry/material layers.

This route prioritizes correct view-dependent PBR response and future asset placement over a
single-view photo projection. It does not upgrade hidden-side confidence: bottom/rear surfaces are
still symmetric design inference.
